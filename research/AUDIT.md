## 2. How much history the engines had

The live app asks Bitget for 500 hourly bars. The study starts entering after 168, so the first 332 of 777 entry hours (43%) gave the engines between 168 and 499 hourly bars, and the rest the full 500.

## 3. The three tests are not independent

They use the same hours and contracts. At 50x a stop 2.5% away lies beyond the liquidation price and can never fill, so a 50x plan in the Day test and in the Naked test is the same position as filed: 1,881 of the 1,881 Day liquidations at 50x are the same events as Naked ones. The 8,332 as-filed liquidations summed over the three tests are 4,671 distinct positions (contract, hour, side, leverage).

## 9. Is "no re-routed flight was liquidated" the stop or the leverage?

The leverage. Flying every Naked plan at its re-routed leverage but without the stop the re-route adds, 0 of 32,634 were liquidated. And the default plan as filed at 10x and 20x, with its own stop, had 0 liquidations in 48,192 (subject to section 4). In this sample, staying at or under about 12x was enough to avoid liquidation on its own.
